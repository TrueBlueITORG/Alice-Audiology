Our Story

At Alice Audiology, we are proud to be Alice Springs' trusted independent hearing clinic, providing personalised and professional hearing care to our local community since 2011.

Located in the heart of Alice Springs, our clinic is dedicated to delivering exceptional hearing services with a genuine focus on compassion, connection and individual care. We understand that hearing is deeply personal, which is why every client who walks through our doors is treated with the time, respect and attention they deserve.

As a proudly independent clinic, we are not limited to one manufacturer. We work with all major hearing aid brands, allowing us to offer unbiased recommendations and customised hearing solutions chosen specifically for you — not for a sales target. We believe better hearing changes lives.

Services / Specialist Services

Specialist Services

Beyond the hearing test — specialist clinical care for tinnitus, balance, processing and ear health.

Specialist care is hands-on, unhurried and explained as we go.

Tinnitus Management

Tinnitus — commonly experienced as ringing, buzzing or noise in the ears or head — is a highly individual condition. We provide comprehensive assessments and personalised management strategies to identify the cause, reduce the impact of symptoms and support your long-term hearing wellbeing.

Balance Testing

Your ears play a crucial role in balance, movement and spatial awareness. Our specialised balance assessments combine advanced diagnostic technology with expert clinical care to identify the cause of dizziness, vertigo or imbalance and help you regain confidence and stability.

Auditory Processing

Evaluation for children and adults who hear sound but struggle to make sense of speech, particularly in noisy classrooms and workplaces.

Industrial Noise Management

Workplace hearing programs for local industry — baseline testing, monitoring and advice on protecting crews from noise exposure.

Hearing Monitoring

Regular review for people at risk of hearing change, including those on ototoxic medication or working in ongoing noise.

Custom Hearing Protection

Individually moulded plugs for work, shooting, music, motorcycling and swimming — comfortable enough to actually wear.

Wax Management

Excess or impacted ear wax can cause hearing difficulties, discomfort and pressure. We remove it using advanced microscope-guided microsuction — gentle, precise and highly effective. We are the only independent audiology clinic in Alice Springs offering this specialised service.

Our independent approach

No sales targets. No preferred brand.

Our hearing aid services are designed to provide truly independent advice, giving you access to a wide range of hearing and listening devices from leading suppliers across Australia, including the latest in cutting-edge hearing technology.

From personalised device selection and precise fitting to fine-tuning, reviews and ongoing support, our clinicians are committed to finding the right solution for your individual hearing needs and lifestyle. Lyric Premier Certified Clinic

Hear naturally. Live freely.

A completely invisible hearing device, worn continuously for months at a time.

Placed once. Then simply forgotten.

Lyric is the world's first completely invisible hearing device. Worn continuously for months at a time, it requires no daily handling and no battery changes, and stays comfortably in the ear while showering, sleeping, exercising and throughout everyday life. Much like contact lenses transformed vision correction, Lyric has revolutionised hearing care — delivering clear, natural sound in a device so discreet it is virtually invisible.

Completely invisible

Nothing behind the ear, and nothing for anyone else to notice.

Continuous wear

Worn 24 hours a day for months at a time, on a simple subscription.

No daily handling

Nothing to fit, clean or put away — useful if dexterity is a challenge.

No battery changes

We replace the device in clinic when it's due. That's the whole routine.

Designed for everyday life

Showering
Sleeping
Exercising
Talking on the phone, at any hour

Lyric isn't right for every ear. We assess suitability at your first appointment and tell you honestly either way.

Scale

Smaller than you are picturing

The device itself

Smaller than a fingertip, the yellow Lyric device is sized and placed for your ear canal by an audiologist — nothing rests on or behind the ear.

Where it sits

Close to the eardrum, which is what gives Lyric its natural sound and invisibility.

In clinic

How a Lyric fitting runs

01

Suitability check

We measure and examine your canals — not every ear suits Lyric, and we say so plainly.

02

Placement

Fitted in a single appointment, with no moulds and no waiting on an order.

03

Wear it and forget it

Months of continuous wear, including showering and sleeping.

04

Replacement

Come in when it is due and we place a fresh device — that is the whole routine.
